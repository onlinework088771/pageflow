==================================================
CHANNEL-BASED ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

The project supports multiple YouTube channels.

Every:

• Upload
• Schedule
• Queue
• Automation
• History

belongs to one specific YouTube Channel.

Filtering should always use the stored YouTube Channel ID.

Never rely on channel names.
